Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies?
No! We bring all necessary cleaning supplies and equipment. We use professional-grade, eco-friendly products that are safe for your family and pets.
Are you insured and bonded?
Yes, Ultra Beam Services is fully licensed, insured, and bonded. We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage for your complete peace of mind.
What's included in a standard cleaning?
Our standard cleaning includes dusting all surfaces, vacuuming and mopping floors, cleaning bathrooms (toilet, shower, sink, mirrors), kitchen cleaning (counters, sink, appliances), trash removal, and general tidying.
How long does a cleaning take?
Cleaning time varies by home size and service type. A standard clean for a 2-bedroom home typically takes 2-3 hours. We'll provide an estimated time when you book.
What if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning?
We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with any aspect of our service, contact us within 24 hours and we'll return to address your concerns at no additional charge.
Do you offer recurring cleaning services?
Absolutely! We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly recurring services with discounts: 15% off weekly, 10% off bi-weekly, and 5% off monthly services.
Do you offer after-hours cleaning for businesses?
Yes! We understand businesses need flexible scheduling. We offer after-hours and weekend cleaning for commercial clients. Contact us for a custom quote tailored to your schedule.
